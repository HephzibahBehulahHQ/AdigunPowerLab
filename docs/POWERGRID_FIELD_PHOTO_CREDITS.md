## Conditions and limits

- Each source record identifies the asset as Public Domain. DVIDS's [Public Use Notice](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) warns that some imagery may have third-party intellectual-property interests, that depictions do not waive individual privacy or publicity rights, and that use must not imply government endorsement. The gallery credits each source and displays a non-endorsement notice.
- The transmission tower is explicitly a storm-damage case, not a normal operating condition. The Tooele wind photograph is a historic facility-scale example; its 2016 source caption describes planned commissioning, so the image does not establish current operating status. The Fort Randall transformer image documents delivery, not commissioning or connection details.
- Optimized image copies are stored under `public/assets/powergrid/field-photos/` and served locally for offline use. The linked source records provide full context.
- Photographs do not establish safe work practices. Use the separately documented schematics and published equipment drawings for topology; follow site-specific safety controls for physical equipment.
