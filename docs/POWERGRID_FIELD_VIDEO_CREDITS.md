# POWERGRID field video credits and context

These four streamed DVIDS records add real hydropower facility footage to the lab. The records mark the videos Public Domain. Titles and captions are limited to what each source states; the clips are documentary material, not live telemetry, measured generation traces, terminal evidence, or proof of current site conditions.

## Playback and reuse

- Video playback uses the official DVIDS embed and requires an internet connection. The linked records remain available if an embed is blocked or unavailable.
- DVIDS's [Public Use Notice](https://www.dvidshub.net/about/copyright) warns that some visual information may have third-party intellectual-property interests, that depiction does not waive personal privacy or publicity rights, and that reuse must not imply government endorsement. The source is credited on each card and a non-endorsement note appears beside the players.
- The footage must not be used as a basis for equipment ratings, model values, wiring topology, operating instructions, or safety procedures.
